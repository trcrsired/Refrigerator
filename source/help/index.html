Quick add

Enter a name, an amount with an SI unit, a price with a currency, and when it was added and expires. The two date fields start at the current time; expiry starts at 7 days. Use the Now and +7d buttons to reset them.

Dates

Both dates accept either an ISO 8601 timestamp (for example 2026-11-27T12:00:00Z) or a Unix timestamp in seconds or milliseconds. The ISO/UNIX button switches the format used for input and display.

Units and price per kilo

Only SI units are offered. Litres and counts are normalised at 1 L ≈ 1 kg so everything can share one price/kg sorting scale.

Storage and alerts

Items can be in the Fridge, Freezer, Pantry or Other — anything can be tracked, not just chilled food. Press Enable alerts to get notifications for items expiring within 24 hours or already expired. Notifications and offline caching depend on your browser's support.
